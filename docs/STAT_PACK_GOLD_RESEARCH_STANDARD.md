# Stat Pack — Gold Research Standard

The Stat Pack is not a list of available counts. Its job is to discover what is historically remarkable about the selected fixture using the LUFC database alone.

## Core research loop

1. Establish the authoritative population.
2. Calculate the current fact.
3. Treat that fact as a research seed, not the finished story.
4. Search the complete relevant LUFC history for the record, rank, predecessor, rarity or exception.
5. Prefer the deepest meaningful historical implication.
6. Suppress technically unique but editorially trivial combinations.
7. Write the result in concise third-person British football language.

## Editorial hierarchy

RECORD → CLUB-HISTORY FIRST → HISTORICAL RANK → RARE MEMBERSHIP → LONG-DISTANCE SINCE → SHORT-DISTANCE SINCE → ORDINARY TREND.

Ordinary first-since comparisons under 12 months normally do not deserve a premium slot. Five years is useful, 10–25 years is very strong, 25+ years is exceptional. Records, first-ever findings and genuinely rare rankings can override recency.

## Gold research families

- Opponent history: winning/losing margin, scoring magnitude, clean sheets, fixture sequences.
- League doubles: completed by Leeds or suffered by Leeds; previous occurrence and historical gap.
- Geography: city/region form, clean sheets, wins/defeats, player records, season concentration.
- Player-conditioned team records: starts/appearances/scoring/assisting linked to team W-D-L and streaks.
- Contemporary leaderboards: meaningful time window + competition + metric + historical rank.
- Goal-type shares: denominator-aware historical ranking with transparent sample thresholds.
- Natural temporal windows: season, calendar year/month, managerial reign, first start, return from absence where database-authoritative.
- Compound stories: merge compatible findings when the combination tells one stronger story.

## Population rules

No fuzzy scope. Opponent, competition, venue, physical stadium, geography, season and player population must use authoritative database fields. Geography must never be inferred from opponent or stadium text. Physical stadium aliases remain governed by the signed-off stadium identity layer.

League double = same opponent + same league season + Leeds wins both H and A. Double suffered is the exact inverse.

## Language

Prefer the historical implication over the arithmetic or query mechanics.

Good: `Leeds are looking to record consecutive home league wins against Brighton for the first time since 2013.`

Avoid: `Leeds can achieve a 2nd consecutive home win in this fixture population.`

Good: `Leeds have kept just two clean sheets in their last 41 competitive matches in London.`

Avoid: `Leeds have 2 shutouts from the previous 41 recorded city-filtered fixtures.`

Never use supporter voice (`we`, `our`, `us`) in generated Stat Pack copy.

## Absolute provenance rule

Every published number, comparator, date, ranking, record and population comes from LUFC Data. External sources may be used only to audit or enrich the database itself and never directly feed a Stat Pack claim.
